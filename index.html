Legal workflow

Identify the matter

Legal authorizes case scope, custodians and responsive data sources.

Preserve & collect

Purview eDiscovery case holds and gathers content under approved procedures.

Review & minimize

Legal validates scope, privilege, sensitivity and redaction needs before export.

Governed handoff

Authorized package, metadata and chain-of-custody details move to RelativityOne.

Review & retain

RelativityOne supports downstream legal review; disposition follows matter policy.

Classification signals for a telecom

Combine label taxonomy, content detection, context and policy scope

◈

Content identifiers

CPNI, subscriber account / contact data, call detail records, payment cards, IMSI / IMEI, IP addresses, credentials and custom data patterns.

▦

Business classification

Public · Internal · Confidential · Restricted. Add telecom-specific sublabels such as CPNI, Network Sensitive, Legal Privileged and M&A.

⛨

Protection settings

Encryption, user / group rights, content markings, container labels, default labels, mandatory labeling and downgrade justification.

⌁

Auto-label conditions

Confidence thresholds, sensitive information types, trainable classifiers, exact data match, label inheritance and policy simulation.

⇥

Enforcement locations

Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams and supported endpoints; use policy tips, audit, user justification, restrict or block.

⚖

Records & legal holds

Retention labels and records management feed authorized investigation and eDiscovery case workflows; legal approves exports for RelativityOne.
